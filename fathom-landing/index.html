Built to take the guesswork out of tap water

Three things Fathom does that a jug filter can't.

Daily testing

A sensor in the base checks chlorine, lead and hardness each morning and logs the results in the app.

Change on time

Fathom counts what has actually passed through the cartridge, so you replace it when it's used up, not on a fixed date.

Fits under a cupboard

At 28 cm tall, it slides under standard wall units and refills from the tap in about 20 seconds.
